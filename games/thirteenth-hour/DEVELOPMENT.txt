IMPORTANT PHYSICAL DESIGN
Lamp: connected 180x90 mm, fold right half behind left at 90 mm.
Wall: FLAT 180x110 mm front mask, NOT a sleeve with folded-back flaps. Keeping
the front mask flat covers the inactive clock row. Three windows are 28x28 mm,
centred x=35/90/145 mm, y=55 mm. Connected strip 160x90 mm; face centres
x=25/80/135 mm, row heights 70 mm (BEFORE) and 25 mm (AFTER).
Align strip 10 mm from frame left. Initial strip bottom = frame bottom -15 mm;
move UP 45 mm, ending strip bottom = frame bottom +30 mm. Both state labels
appear on the protruding grip. The hands and numbers fit within each window.
Hold the frame side margins; no tape, rails or fastener is required.
Drive: two 80 mm circular wheels, 180x100 mm cover/backing. Centres x=45/135,
y=45 mm. Windows x=30/120,y=60,w=30,h=20 mm. Fixed bridge y=67 mm.
Selected correct sector bridge is +22 mm relative to wheel centre; wrong
sectors are +/-5 mm. BOTH equally wrong-height bridges still fail the FIXED
middle line. This is a manually held alignment illustration, not a self-locking
gearbox. The host checks labels and the join before allowing the wall to move.
Cabinet: connected 180x110 mm. Fold last 60 mm behind; special 13 is present
from the beginning, hidden by this fold. Host permits opening only after six
checked findings. Three numbered tracks take loose pointers or pencil circles.

CONTENT / REUSE
Tool A is SQUARE/2/LEFT. Revealed mounting arrow is RIGHT; these are different
operations. BEFORE readings 3,7,10 minus fast-by 1,2,3 yield historical 257.
M-01 maps 257 to LEAF. Drive RIGHT+LEAF passes; wall becomes 6,9,2.
Same corrections produce CURRENT 5,7,11 (2-3 wraps to 11). Never erase 257.
Final proof uses W-01+M-01, AFTER+R-01 and the exposed AFTER tab. The special
13 is a commemorative chime, not ordinary twelve-hour time arithmetic.
