# Linked custom paper-pack quality contract

This version is snapshotted as `packExperienceVersion: 3` for new creator-v3 purchases and newly started checked-pack remakes. An already approved order keeps its saved specification and published files. A failed replacement never overwrites a downloadable pack.

## Narrative and puzzle contract

1. The confirmed five-checkpoint plan fixes each paper activity. The model supplies theme-specific context; programmatic rules create the facts, unique answer, result digits and final code.
2. One unresolved story problem and one concrete final action form the spine. Four links must name the selected printed finding and explain its causal contribution to that action. Evidence and witness links must also cite both printed record facts. Checkpoints 1-4 cannot claim the mission was already completed.
3. Evidence and witness candidates are comparable written records. The two observations describe record contents (`Mentions` or `Names`) and must be meaningful as YES or NO for every candidate. The model cannot compare a clock-face property against a paper note.
4. The final three choices are comparable, plausible actions. The selected action names a specific repair or act; a generic restatement such as “Restore the bell” is rejected. Children must use earlier findings and the four saved digits to select it.
5. The Player Pack prints five separate challenge/material pairs and an ending that recounts the four findings. The adult guide prints the correct result, two hints, wrong-choice recovery and the causal story link at each checkpoint.

## Bounded delivery

- Reject an invalid blueprint before checkpoint generation. Repair a failed checkpoint context together with its one narrative link; a defective finale repairs checkpoint 5 and the spine. Keep the other checkpoint contexts fixed.
- Save the validated blueprint and each deterministic checkpoint on the paid order. Reuse saved work after an upstream, PDF or storage failure. A full narrative audit checks the printed facts and story chain after independent puzzle proof.
- Render and verify both files before publication; publish only after storage checksums match. Keep earlier files available throughout a replacement attempt.
- If bounded repairs cannot pass, leave the order for a human quality review or refund-policy review. Do not publish a weak pack or ask for another checkout.

Local tests use invented Home and Classroom stories, both paper sizes, independent puzzle proof, full queue/database/storage simulation and actual PDF rendering. They do not establish the success rate of the configured provider or prove that unfamiliar families can play without help. A private provider smoke on several themes and physical play-tests are still release evidence.

## Release status, 2026-10-15

This candidate is **not cleared for paid production**. The real-provider validation branch `codex-linked-pack-20261015` has not completed all four invented themes. The latest complete run failed the independent narrative audit for three themes and stopped at a key-word format check for the fourth. Mechanical format failures have since been normalized locally, but the story failures remain. Do not publish version 3 or claim that its generated packs are deliverable until a new provider run produces valid Player and Guide PDFs for every theme and a human checks that the printed game can be played from the supplied pages. A failed run must not consume a customer's plan-edit allowance or replace their existing files.
