# C10 Weather Observatory - morning bulletin edition

This is the current fixed Weather Classroom game contract. The previous
candidate-card/date-grid/symbol-set source in `retail-games-v1.json` is
superseded for C10. Preserve the existing `weather/classroom` URL, offer,
versioned entitlement and order-access identity.

## Play promise

One classroom crew of 2-6 pupils operates printed paper instruments, repairs
one copied entry and authors a checked observation bulletin. For up to 35
pupils, use balanced crews, each with a complete Player Pack. No other crew
holds a required clue. This is invented observation data, never a real
forecast or a weather-safety instruction.

| Mission | Distinct student action | Printed props | Correct outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 / time | Check station seal AND time; sort slips | Five marked slips; three-row board | OUR records 4/9:00, 3/10:00, 1/11:00. Records 2 and 5 have only one bar. |
| 2 / wind | Turn the whole snapshot until its N matches the base compass N; read the thick pointer | Large compass and three square snapshots | 9:00 W; 10:00 W; 11:00 N, for where the pretend wind CAME FROM. |
| 3 / cloud | Count patterned panes and shade matching four-cell record | Three time-labelled four-pane sky cards | 9:00 1/4; 10:00 2/4; 11:00 3/4. |
| 4 / rain | Align strip baseline with gauge zero; read top; correct copied notice | Gauge, three equal-size level strips, copied notice | 0, 1, 3 mm; 11:00 copy of 1 mm becomes 3 mm. |
| 5 / bulletin | Fill blank three-row notice and peer-check each entry against original props | Blank bulletin and five-point audit | Nine readings correct, one documented correction, no unsupported future forecast. |

The three weather datasets are printed in shuffled order and remain separate
from the adult answer atlas. Each mission has a player scene and a matching
adult read-aloud. The teacher releases the next pages only after pupils record
the current result. The story is cumulative; it does not require an arbitrary
code from one instrument to unlock another.

## Print and handout contract

Player pages: 1 cover/quick start, 2 six job descriptions, 3 whole-page
observation board, 4 five station slips, 5 compass base, 6 three wind squares,
7 sky count board, 8 three sky cards, 9 rain gauge, 10 three strips and copied
notice, 11 blank bulletin, 12 peer check and display. One 12-page Player Pack
per crew. Teacher Guide: 10 pages, one shared adult copy or screen view.

Full-cut preparation is 14 outer pieces per crew: five slips, three wind
squares, three sky cards and three rain strips. Low-cut preparation needs only
three wind squares and three rain strips; the slips and sky cards may remain
on intact sheets. No interior holes, glue, fastener, transparent overlay,
device, real instrument or purchased prize is needed. Use ordinary paper,
pencils and adult-handled scissors.

The baseline-to-top distance on rain strips uses the same 28-point spacing as
the printed 0-4 mm base scale. Different paper sizes have the same data and
game steps. Print single-sided at Actual size; never shrink-to-fit. Confirm
the geometry by rendered-page inspection and physical test printing before
claiming physical print validation.

## Teacher acceptance

The guide names exact pages to distribute, role rotation for 2-6 pupils,
one read-aloud before each mission, actions, answer, three graduated hints,
recovery, handover and ending. Pupils can point or dictate; older pupils can
explain why the copied rain entry failed. A different pupil audits the final
notice. The teacher does not invent props or withhold a necessary rule.

First-use repair: the wind props and story consistently call the alignment
symbol an N mark. A neutral E-reading practice drawing fills the spare quadrant
on Player page 6, while a neutral 2 mm alignment drawing uses the spare space
on page 10; neither is a cut piece or a game answer. Player page 2 and Guide
page 3 assign all six jobs explicitly for crews of 2-6, rotate bundles after
each mission, and keep the final Checker distinct from the Recorder. The five
peer-audit rows on Player page 12 have tick boxes and evidence-page fields.
Action labels on the cut props are enlarged. The final Teacher Guide page adds
an optional evidence-defense challenge for older pupils without changing the
five core missions or the fixed answers.

Automated PDF geometry, extracted-text parity, bundle integrity and screen
rendering are necessary local checks, but do not establish physical print or
an unfamiliar-teacher classroom playtest.
