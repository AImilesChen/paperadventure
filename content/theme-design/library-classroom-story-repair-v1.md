# C09 · Storybook Library · The Missing Final Chapter

This is the fixed ready-made Classroom game. Its C09 identity, title, URL and existing order entitlement remain unchanged. `scripts/library_classroom_story.py` is the authoritative source for the four printable files; the older generic `retail-games-v1.json` C09 record is superseded for PDF generation. Run the focused checker after rebuilding.

## Promise and distinctive play

Each crew repairs one short paper story. The five actions differ from the other Classroom worlds: compare a return stamp, sequence four narrative leaves, listen to three private witnesses, make a four-stop route copy and author the missing action, then read the story and correct a visitor’s mistaken summary. There are no candidate ending cards. A teacher can accept varied wording when the causal facts agree with the printed story.

The inner book is *The Lantern Path*. Its return slip has a crescent over two dots; only spine L-24 has both marks. The middle leaves are intentionally printed out of order as C/A and D/B; the correct narrative sequence is B → D → C → A. The opening leaf includes Rowan’s borrowed four-stop map. The three witness notes each carry a separate constraint: Kit needs a path home, Rowan must return the only borrowed map, and the garden keeper has blank paper and pencil. A valid final leaf puts the four stops on Kit’s separate route strip, gives Kit that copy and returns the original map, without inventing an unprepared rescuer or tool. The crew reads the opening, ordered leaves and authored last page, then corrects a false statement that Kit kept the library original.

## Physical inventory and handout order

- Player Pack: 11 pages per crew of 2–6 children. Pages 1–2 are the start and continuing record. Page 3 is the whole return-slip and spine sheet. Page 4 is the opening and original route map. Pages 5–7 are a letter-only sequence board and four large story leaves. Page 8 is the witness evidence board; page 9 holds **three separately cut private notes**. Page 10 has the damaged final leaf, four-stop route-copy strip and ruled writing space. Page 11 is the read-aloud and visitor-correction activity.
- Teacher Guide: 10 pages for the adult, including exact print and cutting inventory, group roles, five read-aloud scenes, acceptance checks, three graduated hints per mission, handovers, a model ending and recovery instructions.
- Print one complete Player Pack per crew. For 35 pupils, use five crews of six and one of five. Keep one Teacher Guide for the adult. Only the three witness slips require cutting; role cards and story leaves may remain on their sheets. In the no-cut mode children write leaf letters in the sequence slots, then turn between whole story sheets for the final reading. Paper, pencil and eraser are enough during play. Short route labels (table, gate, bridge, home) fit the page-10 strip.
- The adult gives pages in the Guide's numbered piles. Do not give page 9 as a whole sheet or expose page 10 before the witness discussion.

## Print and release checks

The build emits Player Pack and Teacher Guide PDFs in A4 and US Letter, plus one two-file ZIP per paper size. Screen-render each page, check margins and cut boundaries, run `scripts/check_library_classroom_story.py`, then run the full retail manifest checker and site/backend tests. Screen inspection cannot establish how an unfamiliar teacher and children will handle a physical printout; collect that feedback after lawful distribution and refine wording if needed. Do not claim a measured play time before that check.
