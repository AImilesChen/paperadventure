# Space Home: The Lost Space Signal, control-room edition

This document supersedes the older `space/home` candidate-card and star-route
rows in `retail-games-v1.json`. The printable source is
`scripts/space_home_control_room.py`; Space Classroom remains a separate game.

## Player promise

One adult can print one complete Player Pack for a team of 2-6 children ages
6-12, cut the marked outer rectangles, and run five paper-only missions using
the Host Guide. For 7-12 children, print two complete Player Packs and form two
teams. Children may point or dictate; no phone, live electricity, real space
knowledge or external prop is needed.

## Causal chain and answer contract

| Mission | Physical action | Verified result | Used later |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Tune | Join three wave strips from HIGH back to HIGH | R-T-K, MID, three waves | Band and phase clues |
| 2. Receive | Place six reusable cells on the device board | Receiver 2, decoder 2, shield 2 | Reconfigure same six cells in mission 5 |
| 3. Tune receive channel | Share three private clues; circle band, phase and the pictured badge | MID / 3 / TRIANGLE | Phase selects code strip; all three fields enter reply |
| 4. Decode | Match received picture order against a differently ordered fixed key and the selected phase strip | SEND 3 | Pulse count for mission 5 |
| 5. Send | Move cells to send state; complete form and use three of five pulse markers | Transmitter 3, shield 2, timer 1; three pulses | Adult releases Pip response |

The Host Guide must require visible props and a completed record row before
releasing the next mission. Hints can disclose an answer but cannot skip the
paper action or the outgoing transmission.

## Print and release inventory

Player pages 1-14; guide pages 1-10. A4 and US Letter are generated separately.
One pack has 24 cut pieces: four jobs, three wave strips, six larger power cells,
three private clues, three code strips and five pulse markers. Keep task pages,
receiver board, power board, calibration board, symbol board, record,
transmission form and Pip response whole. The adult gives pages 1-3 at opening,
then task/prop pairs 4-5, 6-7, 8-9, 10-11 and 12-13. Page 7 and its six cells
remain in play through mission 5. Page 14 stays hidden until the send state and
form are accepted.

Player pages must not print the R-T-K strip order, MID / 3 / TRIANGLE settings,
SEND 3 instruction or final power allocation before players derive them. The
received icon order differs from the fixed key order, so the selected code
strip cannot be read straight across as the solution. Four jobs have explicit
allocations for 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 players. Each mission starts with a voiced
story scene and a discussion prompt; the guide supplies a result-dependent
handover line. Customer PDFs contain play instructions, not internal QA status.
