# Custom paper pack delivery contract v4

This contract is snapshotted as `packQualityVersion: 5` and `packLayoutVersion: 4` for new creator-v3 purchases and eligible same-order rebuilds.

## Causal story frame

- The game begins with one unresolved problem and ends with one concrete action.
- Checkpoints 1-4 each produce an already learned fact. A checkpoint cannot end with an instruction to ask, inspect, find, check, learn or reveal something later.
- Every discovery states why it matters using an explicit consequence. At least three discoveries directly name an object, condition, order, route or location used by the intended final action.
- The fifth checkpoint combines the four independently proved result digits. The numeric code confirms the action; it is not the only story reason for the action.

## Play and puzzle rules

- The first four checkpoints use at least three paper activity types and four distinct deterministic puzzle signatures.
- Every answer is proved from the printed material, has one unique result and requires no outside knowledge.
- Printed labels, adult answers, story links and ending use the same normalized names.
- Failed checks repair only the causal frame, affected checkpoint context or closing. Earlier proved checkpoints remain saved.

## Ending and guide layout

- The ending shows the chosen action, its visible result and a character or community response without inventing a new clue or cause.
- A split final guide keeps the four discoveries, action explanation and next-page instruction together.
- Spare space on final guide pages becomes a labelled host-notes area. A continuation page cannot contain only an orphaned paragraph.

## Publication gate

Both PDFs remain private until deterministic puzzle proof, causal-story validation, independent editorial review, PDF layout checks and storage verification pass. A failed replacement never overwrites an earlier downloadable pack and never creates a new charge.
