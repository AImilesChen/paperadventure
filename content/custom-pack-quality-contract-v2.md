# Custom paper pack quality contract v2

This contract applies to new creator v3 purchases and new remakes of a checked paper pack. An existing delivered pack keeps its original PDF files and saved rules until a replacement passes all checks and is published on that order.

## Game structure

- The approved five-checkpoint plan fixes the play style and paper activity at every checkpoint. The model supplies story-specific labels, short property names, a code word and a closing sentence; it cannot change the rules, answer or required materials.
- Checkpoints 1-4 collect one result digit each. Their scenes and printed labels must not say the mission has already succeeded. Checkpoint 5 combines the four saved digits to select the final action. The ending follows that action.
- The first four paper actions must include at least three distinct material types. Evidence cards compare two printed facts; witness records combine two independent observations; order tiles obey printed relations; symbol keys decode four rows; route boards must offer one traversable two-link path.
- Each material has three distinct answer choices and one provable solution. An independent checker reads the printed facts and choices, verifies the result digit and final four-digit code, and rejects ambiguity or missing links.
- The Player Pack contains a cover, five challenge/material pairs and an ending page. The adult guide stays separate and supplies preparation, page release, two hints, the checked answer, wrong-choice recovery and the story transition for every checkpoint.

## Delivery gates

1. Save the approved plan and the generated blueprint on the paid order. Reject invalid or premature-outcome labels before building checkpoints. Repair only the failing checkpoint context when possible.
2. Save each checked checkpoint. Reuse saved work after a provider or storage failure; do not regenerate earlier successful checkpoints just to retry a later step.
3. Assemble the game; run the independent printed-puzzle proof and a narrative audit that asks explicitly whether the goal was achieved before checkpoint 5.
4. Render both PDFs in the selected A4 or Letter size. Verify required pages, answer separation and print fit before publishing replacement files. Keep the existing download available until that publication succeeds.
5. If a bounded repair cannot pass, keep the paid order open for recovery or support review. A failed version is not a delivered replacement and must not request another purchase.

Automated checks prove structural playability and print geometry, not story appeal, age fit, unfamiliar-family comprehension or physical printer results. Those still need representative provider outputs and human play-tests before claiming the custom pack matches a curated theme.
