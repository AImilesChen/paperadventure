# Paper Adventure｜英文网站文案 v1.4

日期：2026-10-09。用途：PRD v1.4 与 UI 设计的**可直接交前端的英文文案**。三套状态文案分开：`preview`（现在线上）、`downloadable_trial`（先让用户下载试玩稿）、`published_sale`（未来正式商品）。上线时由服务端内容状态选择文案，不得把后一状态预先展示为已开放。术语统一：`ready-made pack`＝固定现成包；`custom adventure`＝按设定生成的一局；`Player Pack`＝玩家材料；`Host Guide / Teacher Guide`＝成人答案与组织指南；`online re-download`＝在线重新下载，不是 PDF 使用期限。

## 1. 主导航和首页

桌面页头：`Story worlds` / `How it works` / `Free sample` / `Pricing` / `Help` / **`Sign in`** / 主按钮 `Create an adventure`。手机菜单保留 `Sign in` 与 `My adventures`；登录后把 `Sign in` 替换为 `My adventures`，在账户菜单给出 `Sign out`。未登录用户点击 `My adventures` 时进入邮箱验证，不出现虚构订单。不要仅把“账号预览”放在页脚。

首页眉题：`PRINTABLE STORY ADVENTURES FOR HOME & CLASSROOM`。H1：`Turn their ideas into an adventure they solve together.` 说明：`Choose a ready-made story world or shape a new printable adventure around your group. Each game brings player pages and a separate guide for the grown-up running it.` 主 CTA：`Explore story worlds`；次 CTA：`Try the free Celebration sample`（只有 `downloadable_trial` 已有完整配套文件时启用，否则 `Preview the sample`）。创建器入口：`Plan a custom adventure`。不要在生成开关关闭时写 `Generate now`。

主题长廊标题：`Choose a story world.` 副文：`A different Home and Classroom story lives in each world.` 卡片状态：`Free sample available`、`Ready-made pack available` 或 `Story concept — pack in preparation`，分别由该 setting 的服务端状态决定。世界图是概念插画时 alt 文案写 `illustration`，不写 `pack preview`。

## 3. 免费试玩稿与资料包文案

当 Celebration H01/C01 的四个文件（A4/Letter × 玩家/指南）及其哈希验证就绪，可将状态改为 `downloadable_trial`。页面顶部：`Free Celebration trial pack — Home and Classroom editions.` 说明：`Download the player pages and the matching grown-up guide, print the paper size you use, and try the full five-checkpoint story. This is version 0.1; we may refine clues and layout after feedback.` 下载卡：`Home / Classroom` → `A4 / US Letter` → `Player Pack (PDF)` 与 `Host/Teacher Guide (PDF)`；指南旁：`Keep hints and answers with the adult organiser.` 反馈：`Found a confusing clue or print issue? Tell us what happened without including a child's name or photo.` 试玩无需先登录、先付款或先提交反馈。

现成付费商品在正式发布时写：`One fixed story, ready to print. Your purchase includes the Player Pack and matching adult guide in A4 and US Letter. There is no AI rewrite in this pack.` 价格与权益按 setting 展示。未发布时写：`This pack is in preparation; the illustration shows the story world, not a finished download.` 避免 `tested`、`approved`、`available now` 等无证据措辞。

## 4. 定制、账户和结账微文案

创建器输入旁：`Describe a place, creature or friendly mystery. Please leave out children's names, addresses and other personal details.` 两条提案前：`These are short directions, not a full game or printable files.` 未接通时：`Ideas are temporarily unavailable. Your setup stays on this device; try again later.` 复核页：`A complete printable adventure starts only after a confirmed purchase.` 模型失败：`We couldn't finish this adventure. You have not received a usable pack. Contact support for a correction or refund review; a system fix does not use one of your included revisions.`

登录发信：`If this adult email can receive mail, a sign-in link is on its way. It expires in 15 minutes.` 失效：`This sign-in link has expired or was already used. Request a new one.` 已验证但空书架：`No purchases are linked to this email yet. You can still explore story worlds or download the free sample.` 退出：`You are signed out. Files on this device are unaffected.`

价格脚注：`One-time US-dollar prices before applicable tax. Stripe shows the final total before payment. No subscription or points.` 固定包：`Online re-download for 12 months from confirmed payment.` 定制：`Online re-download for 12 months from first qualified delivery; two successful local revisions within 30 days of that delivery.` 通用许可摘要：`Home is for one purchaser's household. Classroom is for one purchasing teacher's own classes. Files you have lawfully downloaded may still be used within that licence after online re-download ends.`

结账确认按钮必须明确付款义务，例如 `Continue to secure payment`，Stripe 最终页面按实际规则标明总价。订单返回页：`Returning from checkout does not confirm payment. We'll update this page after payment is verified.` 已就绪：`Your files are ready. Choose A4 or US Letter.` 过期：`Online re-download has ended. Previously downloaded files remain usable within your licence.` 退款：`This order was refunded; online file access is closed.`
