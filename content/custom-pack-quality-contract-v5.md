# Custom Pack Quality Contract v5

This contract is snapshotted as `packQualityVersion: 6` and `packLayoutVersion: 4` for new creator-v3 purchases and eligible same-order rebuilds.

## Structured causal frame

Before any checkpoint material is generated, the model returns one unresolved problem, three concrete final actions and four structured discoveries. Every discovery contains:

- `finding`: a complete fact the players have already learned;
- `consequence`: the concrete story effect of that fact;
- `supportsAction`: an object, condition, step or route phrase from the intended final action.

The application validates these fields as data and compiles the visible story link from the exact printed finding. Valid meaning does not depend on the model using a fixed causal keyword such as `because`, `so`, `enables` or `allows`.

## Bounded repair

If one discovery fails before checkpoint generation, only that discovery is requested again. The unresolved problem, all three final actions and the other three discoveries remain fixed. If a later audit finds one causal discovery defect, the same local repair uses the already printed finding and does not regenerate passed checkpoint materials.

## Delivery gate

The replacement remains unpublished until the deterministic paper proof, structured causal validation, independent story audit, PDF validation and private storage checks all pass. The earlier delivered PDFs remain downloadable throughout generation and recovery. A quality-v6 rebuild uses the same paid order and does not create another charge.
