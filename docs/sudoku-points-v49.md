## Implemented

- Two one-time, non-renewing packs: 100 points for USD 2.99 and 300 for USD 6.99. Server-owned SKU, price, quantity, currency and total are checked; client values cannot determine a payment amount.
- Public pack/rules/FAQ page, game wallet and insufficient-balance purchase/free-practice choices, existing Learn menu and account billing links. Checkout saves the board and notes first; unresolved guest/account progress conflicts must be resolved. Returning restores the chosen game. The existing elapsed game clock continues during checkout.
- Adult signed-in account and versioned immediate-supply notice before checkout. An immutable consent snapshot includes the product, amount, notice and published policy versions. Monthly subscriptions are outside phase one.
- Stripe Checkout integration, mode-scoped customer mapping, same-origin authenticated purchase requests, owned order lookup, hosted-checkout URL allowlist and server-side ownership binding. A request nonce, one-open-checkout constraint, Stripe idempotency keys and rate limit protect creation/retries.
- Signed webhook processing and direct return-page reconciliation share atomic credit logic. Durable events and the existing scheduled handler retry interrupted deliveries. Repeated, concurrent and out-of-order notifications do not grant points twice. Already-paid orders can recover while new checkout creation is closed.
- Paid/free balances accumulate; free rewards are used first. Existing save revisions cannot overwrite a concurrent purchase. All new balance changes are recorded; old balances receive a clearly labelled opening entry, without fabricated historical rewards. Existing paid balance is retained through guest progress merging.
- Exact paid grant lots and FIFO consumption support partial/full refund reconciliation. Only unused points from the refunded lot are revoked. Already-consumed portions are flagged for staff review; another purchase, free rewards and game progress are preserved. Refund initiation stays in Stripe/support: no browser monetary-refund or arbitrary manual-credit button was added.
- Private paginated orders and wallet activity, pending/canceled/error/expired/retry states, support order references, and an authenticated admin review panel with immutable staff notes and payment recheck. The admin browser panel was not exercised with a real admin session; API permissions and behavior were tested.
- Additive migration 0051, schema-completion health marker, future release migration/policy publication steps and matching-API gate. Local reviewed policy source v9 includes point purchases and billing data; it is not yet published.
