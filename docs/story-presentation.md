# Phase three: theme-bound paper records and honest story transitions

New purchases save `packNarrativeBindingVersion: 1`. Step delivery, quality 14, delivery policy 2, selected play allocation, finite variants, budgets, source hashes, queues and existing artifacts are unchanged. This adds no model call, audit, migration or customer recovery grant.

The stable baseline is proved and PDF-preflighted first. A second optional local build uses a finite vocabulary selected only from the approved title/theme anchors: railway, clock tower, hotel, forest, space or museum. Its paper records, preparation steps, decoded words, routes and final plan refer to that world. Unknown themes use a short literal approved anchor in headings, retaining their complete approved title, goal and scenes. No arbitrary theme is promised bespoke causality. If the optional edition cannot pass the existing material, story, puzzle or PDF checks, the first proven edition remains available.

`story-presentation.mjs` compiles a deterministic print view from the bound story, approved plan and independently solved paper facts. It never alters rules, candidate details, options, answers, result digits or final codes. Baseline player scenes preserve the exact approved `scene` and explain the activity actually supplied. Older approved plans without a scene use their exact location prefix when available. A decoded word labels its paper record; it is not claimed to reveal an unprinted location, object or event. Players explicitly record the decoded word with its digit. Baseline adult summaries cite actual paper results instead of invented world consequences. Enhanced editions retain their saved scenes, causal links and ending; their existing frame/material requests receive theme-consistency guidance within the same call budget.

The final guide explains that the four saved digits select the action in this paper game. Baseline guides assign the adult a thematic role (for example stationmaster), explain accepting a paper plan, and narrate the approved mission only after that action. Baseline endings use the exact chosen action and full approved goal, without cutting an object, destination or deadline. Known imperative goals use an infinitive construction; other grammar keeps an explicit quotation. Enhanced editions retain their saved physical ending. This is an improvement to the paper briefing fallback, not a claim that every generic record proves a physical story repair.

The presentation policy is included in the delivery specification hash. Both Player and Guide are preflighted together before selecting the presentation. If either role fails, BOTH render the original checked source. Broken source puzzles still fail independent proof; source, artifact and PDF errors are not hidden by optional presentation fallback. Saved delivery copy is left intact. The original bound source is never mutated, and older purchases keep their previous rendering path. All callers of the interactive renderer, including delivery preflight, baseline construction and final publication, use the same selection.
